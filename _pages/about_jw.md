Jiayi Wang is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National University of Singapore. He received his Ph.D. degree from Tsinghua University. His research focuses on data management and machine learning, with particular interests in LLM-powered data analytics. He has published research in leading database and AI venues, including SIGMOD, VLDB, TODS, CIDR, ICDE, and KDD. He is the champion of the ACM SIGMOD 2023 Programming Contest and served as the organizer of the ACM SIGMOD 2024 Programming Contest. He is also the recipient of the Best of SIGMOD 2023 award and the Siebel Scholar Award (2022). He has served on the program committees of leading conferences, including VLDB, ICDE, CIKM, and AAAI, and as a reviewer for IEEE TKDE.
