Jia Zou is an Assistant Professor at Arizona State University (Arizona, USA). She received her Ph.D. degree from Tsinghua University, and she did a post-doc at Rice University. Her research focuses on data management for machine learning and applied machine learning to enhance data integration, query optimization, and database systems. Dr. Zou is a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award in 2022, an IBM Academic Award in 2021, an Amazon Research Award in 2023, a VLDB Best Paper runner-up award in 2019, and a SIGMOD Research Highlight Award in 2020. She has published 40+ papers in top DB venues including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, TKDE, and the VLDB Journal. She has served as PC Vice Co-chair of IEEE Big Data 2020, Local Chair of WSDM 2022, Co-chair of the SIGMOD 2023 and 2024 New Researcher Symposiums, Co-chair of the EDBT 2026 and 2027 Young Researcher Symposiums, and Chair of the Student Travel Award Committee of SIGMOD 2027.
