Arijit Khan is an Associate Professor at Bowling Green State University (Ohio, USA). His PhD is from the University of California, Santa Barbara, USA, and he did a post-doc in the Systems group at ETH Zurich, Switzerland. His research is on data management and machine learning for emerging problems in large graphs. He is an IEEE Senior Member and an ACM Distinguished Speaker. Arijit is the recipient of the IBM Ph.D. Fellowship (2012–13), a VLDB Distinguished Reviewer Award (2022), and three SIGMOD Distinguished PC Awards (2024, 2025, 2026). He is the author of a book on uncertain graphs and over 130 publications in top DB and AI venues including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICLR, KDD, and EMNLP. Dr. Khan’s editorial roles include Co-Editor-in-Chief of Knowledge Engineering Review and Associate Editor of IEEE TKDE and ACM TKDD. His conference service includes co-chairing the ICDE 2025 demonstration track, the KDD 2025 PhD Consortium track, the Australasian Database Conference 2025 program committee, and the ADMA 2026 and DASFAA 2027 tutorial tracks.
