AI agents built on large language models and vector databases retrieve information, call tools, and update shared state. The traces they leave behind record which procedures worked and which tools failed, yet much of this experience is discarded as logs. This workshop examines how data management can help agents reuse that experience. We invite research on retrieval across relational, vector, and graph data, on memory and context that persist across tasks, and on semantic operators that balance cost, accuracy, and reliability. We welcome systems, abstractions, and benchmarks that evaluate the full path from retrieval to action.

For information about previous editions, please visit the [second workshop at ICDE 2026](https://llmvdb2.github.io/) and the [first workshop at ICDE 2025](https://llmvdb.github.io/).
